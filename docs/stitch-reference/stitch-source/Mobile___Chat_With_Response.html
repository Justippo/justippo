Renewal of Aufenthaltstitel

Yes, you can renew your Aufenthaltstitel (Residence Permit), provided you still meet the initial eligibility requirements. This process typically requires an application at your local Ausländerbehörde at least 4–6 weeks before the current expiry.

Requirements
  • Valid Passport
  • Proof of Income/Funding
  • Current Health Insurance
  • Registration (Anmeldung)
Processing

Average processing time: 6-12 weeks. You will receive a Fiktionsbescheinigung if your permit expires while processing.

Can I renew my Aufenthaltstitel?

smart_toy

Renewal of EU Long-Term Residence Permits

Yes, you can renew your Aufenthaltstitel (Residence Permit). Under current EU regulations and national implementation acts, the renewal process depends on the specific category of your permit. Most long-term residence permits issued under EU Directive 2003/109/EC are renewable by right, provided the holder still meets the qualifying criteria.

  • Application Deadline: It is highly recommended to submit your renewal application at least 8 to 12 weeks before the current expiration date.
  • Integration Requirement: For certain permits, proof of linguistic competence (B1 level) and basic knowledge of the legal and social order of the host country may be required if not already established.
  • Economic Stability: You must demonstrate stable and regular resources which are sufficient to maintain yourself and your family members without recourse to the social assistance system.

According to Article 8 of the Directive, the status is permanent, while the physical residence permit card is usually valid for a period of five years and is automatically renewable upon expiry.

description

EU Directive 2003/109/EC

Concerning the status of third-country nationals who are long-term residents. Defines the conditions for granting and withdrawing long-term resident status and the rights associated with it.
